How To Play

  • Eat the food to grow and raise your score.
  • Hard mode speeds things up, Maze adds walls, Infinite wraps edges.
  • Walls and your own body end the run unless the mode wraps.
  • Restart anytime if you want a fresh board.

Manual Check

  • Movement responds instantly and never reverses into itself.
  • Pause and restart both preserve a clean, readable state.
  • Hitting a boundary or your body shows game over.
